One question: Are we controlling our screens, or are they controlling us?

In 2024, approximately 4.8 billion people worldwide use social media platforms – 56.8% of the global population. While these technologies offer connection and community, a growing body of research reveals a darker side: platform addiction.

This website explores the ethical, neurological, and social dimensions of one of the most pressing public health issues of our time. We invite you to scroll consciously.
